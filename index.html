What it is

Call every play

Five play cards, no playbook trees. Pick a run, a quick pass or a shot downfield and watch the drive unfold.

Play defense

Pick what to take away and live with what that leaves open. Stack the box and the pass beats you. Blitz and the toss goes outside you. One call, not eleven-man micromanagement.

A franchise that never resets

Draft, sign, cut, manage the cap. Your league is yours across seasons. No annual wipe.

Ratings that matter

A great line blocks. A great back breaks tackles. The box score always matches what you just watched.

Honest design

No energy timers. One ad, after a game has finished, never during a drive and never during a play. No banners. One $4.99 purchase removes it and raises the franchise limit from one to three, and there is nothing else to buy.

Runs on your phone

No account of ours, no login of ours, no server of ours. The season, the draft and your franchise all run on the device. A connection is needed for the ad and for the leaderboard, and Game Center is optional.
