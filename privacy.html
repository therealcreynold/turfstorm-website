Privacy Policy

The short version

Turfstorm does not collect your personal data and we operate no server. The game runs on your device. There is no account of ours and no login of ours. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is your Daily Drive score and your achievement progress, and only if you choose to sign in to Apple's Game Center.

What we collect

Nothing, and we have no way to. Turfstorm has no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising, no advertising identifier collection and no user accounts. Your league, your rosters and your progress are stored on your device and nowhere else. There is no iCloud container and no sync, so there is nothing of yours anywhere we could read even if we wanted to.

Game Center, which is the one thing that leaves your device

Turfstorm has one daily challenge, the Daily Drive, and a set of achievements. If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your Daily Drive score and your achievement progress to Apple so that the leaderboard can show them. Apple links that information to your Game Center player identity, which is how a leaderboard works.

On the App Store privacy label this is declared as Gameplay Content, linked to your identity, used for App Functionality. It is not used to track you, it is not used for advertising, and it is never sold or shared with anyone. Once it reaches Apple it is held under Apple's privacy policy rather than ours, and we see nothing about you: what we can see is a leaderboard, the same one you can.

Game Center is entirely optional. If you decline the sign in prompt, turn Game Center off, or play offline, every part of the game still works and your score is still saved on your device. The only thing you lose is the leaderboard, and the app tells you so on screen instead of failing.

Data Apple gives us

If you buy something in the app, Apple processes the payment and gives us aggregate, anonymised sales reports. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple also provides aggregate crash and performance statistics if you have opted into sharing them with developers. Those are anonymous and are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Children

Turfstorm is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children and is not part of Apple's Kids Category. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children either. Game Center sign in is handled by Apple and follows whatever restrictions are set on the device.

If this changes

If a future version of Turfstorm introduces anything that collects data, such as advertising or online play, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated at the same time. We will not quietly start collecting data under an old policy.
