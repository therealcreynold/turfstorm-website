Privacy Policy

The short version

Turfstorm does not collect your personal data. The game runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no login and no server of ours that your game talks to.

What we collect

Nothing. Turfstorm has no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier collection and no user accounts. Your league, your rosters and your progress are stored on your device and nowhere else. There is no iCloud container and no sync, so there is nothing of yours anywhere we could read even if we wanted to.

Data Apple gives us

If you buy something in the app, Apple processes the payment and gives us aggregate, anonymised sales reports. We never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple also provides aggregate crash and performance statistics if you have opted into sharing them with developers. Those are anonymous and are governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Children

Turfstorm is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children and is not part of Apple's Kids Category. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children either.

If this changes

If a future version of Turfstorm introduces anything that collects data, such as advertising or online play, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated at the same time. We will not quietly start collecting data under an old policy.
