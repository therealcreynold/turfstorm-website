What it is

Call every play

Five play cards, no playbook trees. Pick a run, a quick pass or a shot downfield and watch the drive unfold.

Play defense

Pick a coverage, take control of one defender, make the stop. One tap, not eleven-man micromanagement.

A franchise that never resets

Draft, sign, cut, manage the cap. Your league is yours across seasons. No annual wipe.

Ratings that matter

A great line blocks. A great back breaks tackles. The box score always matches what you just watched.

Honest design

No energy timers. No forced ads between drives. Everything optional is optional.

Works offline

No account, no login, no server. The whole game runs on your phone.
