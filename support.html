Common questions

Will my franchise be deleted by an update?

No. Saves carry forward across versions, and that is a design commitment rather than a hope: every save file records the schema version it was written with, and the game migrates old saves forward instead of discarding them.

Where is my save stored?

On your device, and only on your device. Turfstorm has no server and does not sync to iCloud, so a franchise lives on the phone it was started on. Deleting the app deletes the save with it, and a new phone starts a new league. Sync is something we want to add; until it is actually in a build we are not going to claim it here.

Does it work offline?

Yes. We operate no server, and the season, the draft and your franchise all run on your device. Two things need a connection: the ad after a game, which is simply skipped when there is no connection, and the Daily Drive leaderboard, which is Apple's Game Center. Offline you still play the drive and still keep your score, and the leaderboard is replaced by a line telling you why it is not there.

Do I have to sign in to Game Center?

No. Declining costs you the leaderboard and the achievements and nothing else. If you are signed in, Turfstorm sends your Daily Drive score and your achievement progress to Apple so the leaderboard can show them. The privacy policy spells it out.

How many ads are there?

One placement, and it is after a game has finished. Never during a drive, never during a play, and never a banner. You will not see one at all in your first three sittings with the game, and after that it is capped: at most one for every two games you finish, at least ninety seconds apart, and no more than four in a single sitting. Those limits are in the game's own code rather than left to the ad network, and there is a test that fails if any of them is broken. Buying Turfstorm Unlimited removes the placement entirely.

Will Turfstorm ask to track me?

No, and it cannot. Turfstorm asks Google for non-personalised ads on every request, everywhere, so there is nothing to ask permission for and the App Tracking Transparency prompt is never shown. That also means Turfstorm does not appear in the iOS Settings app under Privacy and Security, then Tracking: that list only holds apps that asked. You will still see a privacy form from Google in Europe, the UK and some US states, because the rules there require one, and that is a different thing. The privacy policy has the detail.

An ad was inappropriate or broken. How do I report it?

Email info@crindustries.xyz with a screenshot if you can get one, and say roughly when you saw it. Ads come from Google AdMob and we do not choose the individual ones, but we can report a specific creative to Google and we do.

What does Turfstorm Unlimited buy, and how do I restore it?

Two things. It removes the ad that appears after a game, and it gives you two more franchise slots, so you can run three separate leagues at once instead of one. It is a single $4.99 purchase, not a subscription, and there is no currency and nothing consumable. To find it, or to restore it on a new device, open the season screen, tap Franchise 1 in the header, and tap any row with a padlock. Restoring is a button on that screen and needs the same Apple Account you bought it with.

What is the Daily Drive?

One two minute drill a day, the same one for everybody, generated from the date alone. It takes about two minutes and it is where the leaderboard comes from.

Are the teams and players real?

No. Every team, player, city nickname, logo and league name in Turfstorm is fictional and generated by the game. Turfstorm is not affiliated with any real sports organisation.

Something looks wrong in the box score

Please send it. The simulation is deterministic, which means a seed reproduces a game exactly, so a screenshot with the score and the week is usually enough to reproduce the exact play.

Accessibility

Turfstorm honours Reduce Motion. With it on, the ball marker, the first down line, the score and the drive log change without sliding or springing. The play itself still animates, because a football game whose football does not move is not an accessible football game.

Larger text is the honest gap. The interface is laid out at fixed sizes today and does not yet follow the system text size, so Dynamic Type does nothing in Turfstorm. That is on the list. If something is hard to read or hard to hit, tell us and we will fix it.
